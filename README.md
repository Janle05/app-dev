# My Favorite Series: Jujutsu Kiasen

**Jujutsu Kaisen** is a Japanese manga series written and illustrated by Gege Akutami. It was serialized in Shueisha's shōnen manga magazine Weekly Shōnen Jump from March 2018 to September 2024, with its chapters collected in 30 tankōbon volumes. .

## Why I Love It
- **Amazing animation:** Studio MAPPA delivers fast, fluid, and creative fight scenes
- **Smart power system:** Battles rely on clever strategy with Cursed Energy, not just brute strength.
- **Great characters:** Both the heroes and villains are deeply written and memorable.
- **High stakes:** The dark story doesn't hold back on real consequences.
